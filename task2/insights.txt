----------------------------------------------------------------
KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. REVENUE DRIVERS
   Premium Customers generate the highest per-visit revenue.
   Although they are not the largest group, their combination of
   high income and high spending score makes them the #1 revenue
   contributors. Focus on retention for this segment.

2. UNTAPPED POTENTIAL
   Careful Spenders earn as much as Premium Customers but spend
   very little. They represent the biggest growth opportunity.
   Targeted loyalty programs, exclusive deals, or premium
   membership tiers could convert them into high-value customers.

3. LARGEST SEGMENT — BUDGET CUSTOMERS
   Budget Customers form the largest group (~31%). While
   individual spend is low, their volume makes them significant.
   Value-for-money promotions, discount events, and bundle deals
   are the right approach here.

4. HIGH SPENDERS (LOW INCOME)
   This group punches above their weight financially. They respond
   well to buy-now-pay-later options, EMI plans, and emotional
   marketing. Risk: potential churn if credit becomes restricted.

5. AVERAGE CUSTOMERS
   The middle segment is stable and consistent. Standard seasonal
   campaigns and email newsletters work well. They can be nudged
   toward Premium with upsell strategies.
