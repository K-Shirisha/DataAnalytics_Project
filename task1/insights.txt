====================================================
  SALES & REVENUE ANALYSIS — BUSINESS INSIGHTS
====================================================

Dataset: Superstore-style sales data (2022–2024)
Records: 2,000 orders across 3 categories, 4 regions

----------------------------------------------------
1. HIGHEST REVENUE GENERATING CATEGORY
----------------------------------------------------
Technology leads all categories in total revenue.
High-value items like MacBooks, Dell laptops, and
cameras generate significantly higher per-order sales
compared to Furniture and Office Supplies.

----------------------------------------------------
2. BEST-SELLING PRODUCTS
----------------------------------------------------
The top revenue-generating products are:
  - Apple MacBook Pro
  - Leather Sofa
  - Dell XPS 15
  - Herman Miller Chair
  - Canon DSLR Camera

These products have high unit prices and consistent
demand throughout the year.

----------------------------------------------------
3. MOST PROFITABLE REGION
----------------------------------------------------
The East region consistently delivers the highest
profit margins, driven by a higher regional sales
multiplier and strong Technology purchases.

----------------------------------------------------
4. MONTHLY SALES TRENDS
----------------------------------------------------
- Sales show a seasonal uptick towards Q4 (Oct–Dec)
  corresponding to year-end spending and holiday deals.
- Q1 tends to be slightly slower across all categories.
- Technology purchases spike during back-to-school
  months (Aug–Sep) and the festive season.

----------------------------------------------------
5. LOSS-MAKING AREAS
----------------------------------------------------
- Some Office Supplies sub-categories (Binder Clips,
  Sticky Notes) operate at thin or negative margins
  due to high competition and low pricing pressure.
- Discounting in Office Supplies should be reviewed —
  15% of rows in this category record negative profit.

----------------------------------------------------
6. REGIONAL OBSERVATIONS
----------------------------------------------------
- East & West together account for ~55% of total sales.
- Central and South regions present growth opportunities
  with targeted marketing campaigns.

----------------------------------------------------
7. RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------------------
a) Increase Technology category promotions — highest
   margin and revenue driver.
b) Bundle Office Supplies with high-margin items to
   improve blended margins.
c) Invest in East region infrastructure to sustain
   its leading performance.
d) Launch Q1 promotional campaigns to offset the
   seasonal sales dip.
e) Audit loss-making Office Supplies SKUs and
   consider rationalising the product mix.
